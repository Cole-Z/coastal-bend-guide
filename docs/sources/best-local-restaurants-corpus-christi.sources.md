2. **Hester's hours and reservation policy:** not published on the official site.

4. **Republic of Texas:** start time is 5 p.m. per Omni and 5:30 p.m. per OpenTable. The draft uses the Omni times.
5. **Water Street brunch days** are not stated on the menu.
6. **Kid-friendliness for date-night steakhouses** isn't stated by those restaurants. The draft only says they're "geared to adults in the evening."
