## Flagged / uncertain items (resolve before removing draft: true)

1. **Little Shell / Big Shell mile markers** aren't stated, because no NPS page I could reach lists them. Add them only from an NPS map or brochure.
2. **Travel time from Corpus Christi** is only the NPS airport figure (38 mi / 45 min). Times from downtown or the Southside are left vague.
3. **Fishing near Malaquite Beach:** NPS bans fishing in designated swimming areas, but no page says whether Malaquite counts. Ask a ranger. The draft only says to cast away from swimmers.
4. **Fees** are from the NPS fees page (last updated Dec 2025). Federal pass policy changed in 2026, so recheck before publishing. ATB prices are left out on purpose.
5. **Hatchling season** varies year to year. The 2027 dates won't be posted until June 2027.
6. **Opinion/advice in Cole's voice:** bring your own shade, stake it against wind, stay on Malaquite with a baby, arrive early, the trail is stroller-friendly. These are general guidance, not sourced facts.
7. **Personal voice removed** (Cole's standing rule, applied 2026-11-14). The draft has no first-person claims. See the suggested lines below.
8. **Entrance-station hours vary daily**, and the park doesn't publish a schedule.
