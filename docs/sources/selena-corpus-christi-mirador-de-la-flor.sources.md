## Flagged / uncertain items

1. **Fiesta de la Flor 2027:** one unofficial event-listing site shows Apr 23–24, 2027, but no official source (Q-Productions, the family, Visit Corpus Christi, the city) confirms a revival. The festival was officially ended in Jan 2020. The draft says no official 2027 announcement exists, so recheck before publishing.
2. **Cemetery visiting hours** aren't published. Only office hours (8–5 daily) are, so the draft says to call.
3. **Peoples Street T-Head parking fees:** "free" comes only from third parties, so the draft says to check signs.
4. **Statue unveiling year (1997)** comes from third-party sources only.
5. **Hi-Ho hours** come from aggregators and the VCC "2:30 p.m." note. There's no official site.
6. **Museum exhibit and Selena Museum contents** are attributed to Visit Corpus Christi, not confirmed by the venues.
7. **March 31 anniversary date** is widely documented but wasn't read on an official page during this check.
8. **Deliberate omissions for respect:** the private home address, the name of the person convicted, and the location where she was killed.
9. **Advice lines** (go on a weekday, group stops by area, visit the memorial twice, flower etiquette) are general guidance.
