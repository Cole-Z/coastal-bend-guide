# First-weekend itinerary and topic hubs

Checked November 22, 2026. The order of stops, pace, and swaps are editorial suggestions.

| Claim or decision | Official source and verification |
| --- | --- |
| North Beach is across the Harbor Bridge from downtown. The Lexington and aquarium are neighboring anchors. North Beach and McGee are bay beaches. | [City bay beaches](https://www.corpuschristitx.gov/department-directory/parks-and-recreation/beaches/bay-beaches/). |
| Lexington parking and address, admission, hours, five route descriptions, ladder-heavy areas, and hangar/flight deck options. | [Directions and parking](https://usslexington.com/visit/directions-map-how-to-get-to-lex/), [hours and admission](https://usslexington.com/visit/hours-admission/), [self-guided routes](https://usslexington.com/tours/self-guided-tours/). This is a targeted logistics recheck. The [original Lexington ledger](uss-lexington-museum-visit.sources.md) records prior checks across the rest of the article. |
| Aquarium is a marine-exhibits anchor. Admission and hours should come from the operator. | [Texas State Aquarium visitor information](https://www.texasstateaquarium.org/plan-your-visit/). |
| Mirador de la Flor is by the downtown marina and seawall. | [Visit Corpus Christi seawall guide](https://www.visitcorpuschristi.com/blog/post/things-to-do-on-the-corpus-christi-downtown-seawall/). |
| Whitecap is on North Padre Island. City Gulf beach driving and parking guidance applies. | [Gulf beaches](https://www.corpuschristitx.gov/department-directory/parks-and-recreation/beaches/gulf-beaches/), [parking permits](https://www.corpuschristitx.gov/department-directory/parks-and-recreation/beaches/beach-parking-permits/), [beach rules](https://www.corpuschristitx.gov/department-directory/parks-and-recreation/beaches/beach-rules/). |
| Mustang Island State Park has day-use access on SH 361 with state-park fees and reservations. | [Texas Parks and Wildlife](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/mustang-island). |
| Malaquite is reached via Park Road 22. The visitor center has parking and walk-down access to a vehicle-free beach. Check park admission and surf alerts. | [Directions](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/directions.htm), [visitor center](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/visitor-center.htm), [beach subdivisions](https://www.nps.gov/pais/learn/nature/beaches.htm), [fees](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/fees.htm), [conditions](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/conditions.htm). |
| Mainland ferry via Aransas Pass goes to Mustang Island. The JFK Causeway and island stretch of SH 361 offer the road approach. | [TxDOT ferry route and wait-time link](https://www.txdot.gov/discover/ferry-boat-schedules.html), [City Padre/Mustang Island plan](https://www.corpuschristitx.gov/media/0qokvibl/padre-mustang-island-adp.pdf). |
| Downtown indoor museum and outdoor Oso Bay trail options. | [Art Museum visit information](https://www.artmuseumofsouthtexas.org/plan-your-visit/), [City Oso Bay Wetlands Preserve](https://www.corpuschristitx.gov/department-directory/parks-and-recreation/parks-and-facilities/oso-bay-wetlands-preserve-and-learning-center/). |
| Water Street downtown, Hester’s at Six Points and Lamar Park, Kiko’s on Everhart, Doc’s at the causeway. | [Water Street Oyster Bar](https://waterstreetoysterbar.com/hours-location/), [Hester’s locations](https://hesterscafe.com/locations/), [Kiko’s menu and address](https://kikosmexicanfood.com/menu), [Doc’s menu and location](https://docsseafoodandsteaks.com/menu/). The [original food research](best-local-restaurants-corpus-christi.sources.md) covers the longer article. |

Use the operators’ pages for day-of rates, closures, reservations and venue rules. Conflicting Snoopy’s hours from the original food research were left out of the new shortlist. The itinerary uses Doc’s for the causeway stop.
