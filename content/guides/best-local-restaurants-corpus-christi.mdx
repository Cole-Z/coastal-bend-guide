Corpus Christi eats like the coast town it is. Gulf shrimp and oysters are on menus everywhere, breakfast tacos are a way of life, and the best tables come with a water view. This list sticks to established local spots, many of them family-owned and open for decades, sorted by what you're in the mood for.

Hours and policies below come from each restaurant's own website or menu. They change, especially between summer and the off-season, so call ahead before you make a special trip.

## Seafood

**Doc's Seafood & Steaks (Padre Island)**

Doc's sits right on the water under the JFK Causeway bridge, on the way to the Island. It has indoor and outdoor seating, daily live music, and sunset views over the Laguna Madre.

- **Where:** 13309 S. Padre Island Dr. Phone (361) 949-6744.
- **Hours:** 11 a.m. to 9 p.m. Sunday through Thursday, and 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. Friday and Saturday. The Waterline, the downstairs restaurant at sea level, opens at 4 p.m. on weekdays and 11 a.m. on weekends.
- **Reservations:** none during peak season, Memorial Day through Labor Day. The rest of the year, call or send an online request, and they'll try to fit you in. Expect a wait on summer weekends.
- **On the menu:** Doc's lists its Cajun Surf & Turf (blackened NY strip topped with blackened shrimp and a poblano crawfish cream sauce), grilled coconut shrimp, crab cakes, and key lime pie among its best-known dishes.
- **Bring your catch:** coming off the water? Doc's will clean and fillet your fish downstairs, then cook it grilled, blackened, or fried with rice and vegetables. See our fishing and boat-outings guide for places to catch it.
- **With kids:** a kids menu for ages 12 and under ($9 each) includes shrimp, fried flounder, chicken nuggets, mini corn dogs, and cheeseburgers, each with fries, vegetables, or applesauce.
- **With dogs:** dogs are welcome downstairs at The Waterline but not in the upstairs dining room.

**Water Street Oyster Bar (downtown)**

A downtown standby since 1983, Water Street fills a historic former transmission shop in the Water Street Market, steps from the bay.

- **Where:** 309 N. Water St. Phone 361-881-9448.
- **Hours:** 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. Sunday through Thursday, and 11 a.m. to 11 p.m. Friday and Saturday.
- **Reservations:** call ahead to get on the list. For groups of six or more, the restaurant recommends reserving at least 24 hours in advance.
- **On the menu:** raw Gulf oysters on the half shell, wood-fired oysters, oysters Rockefeller, Shrimp Picayune, Seafood Pasta Jambalaya, and market fish. There's also a sushi menu and a daily blackboard of specials.
- **Happy hour:** daily from 3 to 6 p.m., with half-price oysters on the half shell, half-price sushi rolls, and drink specials.
- **Brunch:** a brunch menu served 11 a.m. to 2 p.m., with dishes like eggs Benedict, Eggs Orleans with blue crab cakes, and sourdough pain perdu.
- **With kids:** the posted menus don't include a kids section. It's a casual, busy room, but ask your server about smaller portions if you need them.
- **To go:** the attached Water Street Seafood Company handles takeout and sells fresh fish to cook at home.

**Black Diamond Oyster Bar (Flour Bluff)**

A casual neighborhood oyster bar on NAS Drive in Flour Bluff.

- **Where:** 418 NAS Dr. Phone (361) 353-4210.
- **Hours:** the restaurant doesn't post hours on its own website right now, and listings show it closed Mondays. Call before you go.
- **Good to know:** this is a laid-back oyster-and-seafood stop, not a fancy night out. Check the restaurant's social media pages for the current menu and any live music.

**Snoopy's Pier (Padre Island)**

Snoopy's opened in 1980 as a small bait stand and burger joint on a 600-foot fishing pier. Today it's a casual waterfront seafood spot on the Laguna Madre, next door to Doc's.

- **Where:** 13313 S. Padre Island Dr. Phone (361) 949-8815.
- **Hours:** seasonal. It's generally open from late morning into the evening, with longer hours in summer. Check the website before a late dinner.
- **With kids:** Snoopy's calls itself a family-friendly restaurant. Next door, Scoopy's serves Blue Bell ice cream with a view of the water.

## Tex-Mex

**Kiko's Mexican Restaurant (Southside)**

Family-owned and serving Corpus Christi since 1977, Kiko's covers breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

- **Where:** 5514 Everhart Rd. Phone (361) 991-1211.
- **Hours:** 6:30 a.m. to 9 p.m. Monday through Thursday, 6:30 a.m. to 9:30 p.m. Friday and Saturday, and 7 a.m. to 8:30 p.m. Sunday.
- **On the menu:** Kiko's lists homemade flour tortillas, breakfast tacos, cheese enchiladas, carne guisada, fajitas, Mexican breakfast plates, and margaritas among its best-known dishes.
- **With kids:** a children's plate menu for ages 12 and under has a single taco, enchilada, chalupa, or carne guisada with rice and beans, plus chicken strips and a "Mexi-Burger." Seniors 65 and up can order from the same menu.
- **Good to know:** the restaurant offers online ordering for pickup and has a private dining room for groups.

## Breakfast

**Price's Chef (Six Points area)**

Price's Chef has been a Corpus Christi diner since 1940. Breakfast is served all day.

- **Where:** 1800 S. Alameda St., at Palmero Street. Phone 361-883-2786.
- **Hours:** 7 a.m. to 2 p.m., seven days a week.
- **Reservations:** walk-ins are always welcome. Call ahead for large parties.
- **On the menu:** the Price's Breakfast comes with two eggs, a pancake, ham, sausage, bacon, biscuits, and hash browns. There are also omelets, salads, sandwiches, burgers, and a few steak and chop plates.

**Hester's Cafe (Six Points and Lamar Park)**

Hester's is a scratch café and bakery with two Corpus Christi locations, serving breakfast, brunch, and lunch.

- **Six Points:** 1714 S. Alameda St. Phone 361-885-0558. This is the original café, opened in 2006 in a 1954 hardware-store building.
- **Lamar Park:** 3812 S. Alameda St., in the Lamar Park Center. Phone 361-855-1892.
- **Hours:** daytime only. The cafés don't post hours on their website, so check before you go, especially on Sundays.
- **On the menu:** breakfast and brunch, including griddle dishes like Cloud Cakes, plus sourdough sandwiches, salads, bowls, pastas, a full coffee bar, and a bakery case. Whole cheesecakes and pies need 24 hours' notice.
- **Good to know:** you can order online for pickup from either café. There are several vegetarian options. The café says its gluten-free items aren't safe for people with celiac disease.

**Kiko's** (see Tex-Mex above) opens at 6:30 a.m. on weekdays, making it a solid choice for early breakfast tacos before a beach or fishing day.

## Date night

**Republic of Texas Bar & Grill (downtown, Omni hotel)**

A multilevel steakhouse on the 20th floor of the Omni Corpus Christi Hotel, with panoramic views of the bay. It's a long-standing special-occasion pick.

- **Where:** 900 N. Shoreline Blvd. Phone (361) 886-3515.
- **Hours:** dinner from 5 to 10:30 p.m. Monday through Saturday, and 5 to 9 p.m. Sunday.
- **Reservations:** recommended. Book through the hotel's online reservation system or by phone.
- **On the menu:** prime beef, wild game, and seafood, with a big wine list.
- **Dress code:** business casual. No athletic wear, beachwear, swimwear, flip-flops, or tank tops, so come straight from dinner plans, not straight from the beach.

**Katz 21 Steak & Spirits (Southside)**

A Southside steakhouse that focuses on dry-aged prime beef and Gulf Coast seafood. It has a lounge, Club 21, and an outdoor patio bar.

- **Where:** 5702 Spohn Dr. Phone (361) 884-1221.
- **Hours:** 5 to 10 p.m. Tuesday through Thursday, and 5 to 11 p.m. Friday and Saturday. Closed Sunday and Monday, except for private events.
- **Reservations:** book online. For parties of seven or more, call.
- **Good to know:** a sushi menu, 21 Rollz Sushi Co., is served Tuesday through Saturday.

**Water Street Oyster Bar** (see Seafood above) also works for a more relaxed date night. Happy hour runs 3 to 6 p.m. daily, and the downtown bayfront is a short walk away for an after-dinner stroll.

## Tips for eating out in Corpus Christi

- **Summer and holiday weekends get busy,** especially at waterfront spots on the Island. Go early, or plan for a wait.
- **Ask about kids menus.** Many casual seafood and Tex-Mex spots have one, but fine-dining steakhouses are geared to adults in the evening.
- **Fresh from the water:** if you're fishing, Doc's will cook your catch.
- **Call ahead** if you're going out of your way. Hours here change with the season.
