**Day 1:** Pick the USS Lexington or the Texas State Aquarium on North Beach. Cross the Harbor Bridge for a seawall stroll and dinner downtown. **Day 2:** Cross the JFK Causeway for one Gulf beach. Check the surf forecast before you commit.

## Day 1 — North Beach and downtown

1. **Morning on North Beach.** Pick the [USS Lexington](https://ccbaylife.com/guides/uss-lexington-museum-visit/) for aircraft, ship history, and exploring decks. Steep ladders limit some routes. Pick the [Texas State Aquarium](https://www.texasstateaquarium.org/plan-your-visit/) for a morning centered on marine exhibits. The two venues are next to each other across the ship channel from downtown. One is plenty if you’re traveling with young kids or want an unhurried afternoon.
2. **Afternoon on the downtown seawall.** Cross the Harbor Bridge and walk a short stretch near the marina. Stop at [Mirador de la Flor](https://ccbaylife.com/guides/selena-corpus-christi-mirador-de-la-flor/), the outdoor memorial to Selena along the seawall.
3. **Dinner on Water Street.** Stay downtown for seafood at [Water Street Oyster Bar](https://waterstreetoysterbar.com/hours-location/). See the [Corpus Christi restaurant guide](https://ccbaylife.com/guides/best-local-restaurants-corpus-christi/) for more options.

## Day 2 — Pick one Gulf beach

North Beach and downtown’s McGee Beach face Corpus Christi Bay. For Gulf surf, take South Padre Island Drive (TX 358) across the JFK Causeway. Then pick a stop based on how much time you have, whether you want to keep vehicles off the sand where you’ll walk, and the day’s conditions.

- **Whitecap Beach for a shorter island outing.** It’s a city-managed Gulf beach on North Padre Island. Check the city’s [beach parking permits](https://www.corpuschristitx.gov/department-directory/parks-and-recreation/beaches/beach-parking-permits/) and [beach rules](https://www.corpuschristitx.gov/department-directory/parks-and-recreation/beaches/beach-rules/) before driving or parking on the sand.
- **Mustang Island State Park for a state park beach day.** Head up State Highway 361 from North Padre Island. Use the [Texas Parks and Wildlife park page](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/mustang-island) for day-use fees, reservations, and alerts. Plan a picnic or a shoreline walk.
- **Malaquite for a vehicle-free stretch of sand.** Continue south on Park Road 22 to Padre Island National Seashore. Park at the [Malaquite Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/visitor-center.htm) and walk down to the beach. The national seashore has a separate [entrance fee](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/fees.htm). Pack water and lunch and check [the national seashore day-trip guide](https://ccbaylife.com/guides/padre-island-national-seashore-day-trip/) before you leave.

If you want a meal on the way back, [Doc’s Seafood & Steaks](https://docsseafoodandsteaks.com/menu/) is by the JFK Causeway. For more options, see the [food shortlist by area](https://ccbaylife.com/topics/food/).

## Make the plan fit your group

- **Indoor swap:** Trade the Gulf outing for the [Art Museum of South Texas](https://www.artmuseumofsouthtexas.org/plan-your-visit/) downtown. Check opening hours and exhibitions before setting out.
- **Short with kids:** Do the aquarium or the Lexington’s hangar deck, then pick one brief stop outdoors if conditions allow. The [family outing guide](https://ccbaylife.com/guides/family-things-to-do-corpus-christi/) has playground and indoor options.
- **Budget-friendly:** Pack a picnic, walk the downtown seawall, and see Mirador de la Flor. For a second short outdoor outing, try the [Oso Bay Wetlands Preserve trails](https://www.corpuschristitx.gov/department-directory/parks-and-recreation/parks-and-facilities/oso-bay-wetlands-preserve-and-learning-center/) or pick a [city bay beach](https://www.corpuschristitx.gov/department-directory/parks-and-recreation/beaches/bay-beaches/) and check parking signs.

## Optional Port Aransas outing

Give Port Aransas its own outing. From Corpus Christi, you can drive over the JFK Causeway to North Padre Island and follow State Highway 361 up Mustang Island. That route reaches Port Aransas without a ferry. The mainland ferry approach runs via Aransas Pass and crosses the channel to Port Aransas. Check [TxDOT’s ferry information and wait-time link](https://www.txdot.gov/discover/ferry-boat-schedules.html) before choosing the mainland route.
