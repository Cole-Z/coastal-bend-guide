## What this page is for

A finished fishing guide would explain how people fish this coast, and where to read the current rules. This draft names categories only. It does not tell you what you can keep.

## Topics to verify before publishing

- Categories a guide might use: bay, surf, jetty, and offshore
- Licenses and limits, which are set by Texas Parks & Wildlife — open their current regulations and link that page instead of restating numbers
- Species and seasons an editor who fishes here would want to name, after checking that page
- A pointer to the official marine forecast, not a forecast of our own
- What a responsible charter mention needs: a real business, a way to contact them that they published, and no invented price

## Leave out until you have a source

- Bag limits, size limits, and license fees
- Charter names, trip prices, and catch stories
- Ramp addresses and bait-shop reviews

Remove `draft: true` only after every statement on the page is checked against a source you trust, and set `updated` to the day you checked.
