Nightlife here runs more toward backyard patios, live bands, and a cold one by the water than toward velvet ropes. Most of the action is in three places: downtown near the bayfront, out toward the JFK Causeway and North Padre Island, and across the ferry in Port Aransas.

Hours and details below come from each venue's own website unless noted. Music lineups change weekly, so check a venue's calendar or social media before you head out.

## Downtown: live music and bars

Downtown's bar scene clusters around Chaparral and Water streets, a few blocks from the bayfront. It's easy to park once and walk between spots.

**Executive Surf Club**

A downtown institution since 1990, the Surf Club has an outdoor stage that has hosted touring acts and local bands for decades. It says it has something going on every night.

- **Where:** 306 N. Chaparral St. Phone (361) 884-7873.
- **Hours:** 11 a.m. to 11 p.m. Sunday through Thursday, and 11 a.m. to midnight Friday and Saturday.
- **What to expect:** live music, a long-running open mic, 36 beers on tap, and casual food like burgers. Check the music calendar on the club's website for who's playing.

**Brewster Street Icehouse**

A big, casual icehouse with live music, cold beer, and Texas comfort food. It has three Corpus Christi locations.

- **Downtown:** 1724 N. Tancahua St. Phone (361) 884-2739. Open 11 a.m. to 11 p.m. Sunday through Wednesday, and 11 a.m. to 1 a.m. Thursday through Saturday.
- **Also:** a Southside location at 5550 S. Holly Rd. and an Island location at 15241 Leeward Dr. on North Padre Island. Both close at 11 p.m. Sunday through Wednesday and at midnight Thursday through Saturday.
- **Happy hour:** 3 to 7 p.m. Monday through Friday at all three locations.
- **Good to know:** Brewster Street describes itself as family-friendly, which makes it an easy pick for an early-evening show with mixed ages. Check the music and events page for ticketed concerts.

## Breweries and taprooms

**Lazy Beach Brewing**

A small local brewery with two spots: a downtown taproom and a larger Southside brewery with a taproom and café.

- **Downtown taproom:** 312B N. Chaparral St., right by the Surf Club. Open 5 to 9 p.m. Tuesday through Thursday, 5 to 10 p.m. Friday, 3 to 10 p.m. Saturday, and 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. Sunday.
- **Southside brewery, taproom, and café:** 7522 Bichon, Suite 100. Phone 361-693-5347. Open 5 to 10 p.m. Tuesday through Friday, 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. Saturday, and 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. Sunday.
- **What to expect:** a weekly beer release, rotating taps (most pints listed at $6 to $7), and regular events like salsa nights, food truck days, and patio markets.

**Nueces Brewing Company**

A downtown brewery and taproom on South Water Street, near the bayfront hotels.

- **Where:** 401 S. Water St. Phone 361-356-6404.
- **Hours:** 11 a.m. to 9 p.m. Tuesday through Thursday, 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. Friday and Saturday, and 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. Sunday. Closed Monday.
- **Good to know:** the taproom can also be rented for private events.

**Rebel Toad Brewing Co.**

A small, independent downtown brewery in the Marina Arts District.

- **Good to know:** Rebel Toad says it welcomes families, and dogs on the outdoor patio. There's no kitchen, so bring your own snacks or order from nearby restaurants. It's open evenings, Wednesday through Saturday. Check current hours before you go.

## Waterfront spots

**Doc's Seafood & Steaks and The Waterline (Padre Island)**

Right under the JFK Causeway bridge, Doc's keeps a daily live-music calendar. The downstairs Waterline is a sea-level spot that welcomes dogs. It makes an easy sunset stop over the Laguna Madre.

- **Where:** 13309 S. Padre Island Dr. Phone (361) 949-6744.
- **Hours:** Doc's is open until 9 p.m. Sunday through Thursday, and until 10 p.m. Friday and Saturday. The Waterline keeps similar evening hours.
- See our restaurants guide for more on Doc's.

**The Hemingway Bar at Marker 37 Marina (Padre Island)**

Next door at Marker 37 Marina, this waterfront bar serves craft cocktails and Cuban dishes, with live music on weekends. Boaters can dock right at the bar.

- **Where:** 13317 S. Padre Island Dr. Phone (361) 369-0271.
- **Hours:** noon to 10 p.m. Friday, noon to 11 p.m. Saturday, and noon to 9 p.m. Sunday. Hours may change with the season.

**On the water**

For a night out on the bay itself, the Texas Tease evening cruise leaves from the Peoples Street T-Head downtown, with live music and a full bar. Port Aransas operators run sunset cruises too. See our boat-outings guide for details.

## Port Aransas evenings

Port Aransas (Port A) has a classic beach-town night scene: open-air bars, live music, and a lot of flip-flops. It's on Mustang Island, reached by the free TxDOT ferry from Aransas Pass or by driving up the island from North Padre.

**Shorty's Place**

Billed as the oldest bar in Port Aransas, Shorty's has been open since 1946.

- **Where:** 101 Beach St., Port Aransas. Phone (361) 749-8224.
- **What to expect:** a no-frills local bar with live music. Shorty's posts its music schedule on its website and Facebook page.

**The Gaff**

A pirate-themed bar on Beach Street known for pizza, beer, live music, and belt-sander races.

- **Where:** 323 Beach St., Port Aransas. Phone (361) 749-5970.
- **Good to know:** The Gaff mainly posts updates on its Facebook page. Check there for hours and the live music lineup.

## Practical tips

- **Plan your ride home.** Venues are spread out between downtown, the Island, and Port A, so line up a rideshare, a taxi, or a designated driver before you go out, especially if you're crossing the causeway or the ferry late at night.
- **The Port A ferry runs 24 hours a day,** every day, weather permitting, and it's free. On summer weekends and holidays, waits can stretch past an hour. If you're staying in Port A, it's simplest to stay on the island for the evening.
- **You must be 21 to buy alcohol in Texas.** Bring a valid photo ID even if you're well past 21, because many places card everyone. Each bar sets its own policy on whether under-21s can come in, and some go 21+ at night or for certain shows. Check before you bring teens.
- **Closing time:** by Texas law, bars stop serving at midnight, or 1 a.m. on Saturday nights, unless they hold a late-hours permit in an area that allows it. Late-hours bars can serve until 2 a.m. A bar's posted hours are the best guide.
- **Never drink and drive,** and that includes boats. Texas boating-while-intoxicated laws apply on the water too.
- **Check the calendar.** Most venues post their live music lineups weekly on their websites or social media, and big shows can sell out.
